## LinkedIn / longer

> I got tired of demo review tools that tell you your K/D and then advise you
> to "work on your positioning."
>
> So I built one that reads the actual positions. It parses a FACEIT demo into
> callouts — which one you died in, which one you were killed from, whether a
> teammate was close enough to trade you, what the death cost the round — and
> reviews the match from those.
>
> The part I'm most pleased with is what it refuses to do. The coach never sees
> enemy positions, because you didn't have them either. It won't call a pattern
> a habit until it's shown up across enough matches. It won't tell you the enemy
> picked up your dropped rifle, because the demo doesn't actually record that.
>
> Everything it says, you can go and count yourself.
